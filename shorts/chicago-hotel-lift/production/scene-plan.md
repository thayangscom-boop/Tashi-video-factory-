**Pacing:** something visibly changes every 2–4 s, whether a camera move, a cut, a pop-up label or a character action.
**Safe zones:** captions sit at roughly y = 1290–1500 px, which keeps them clear of the top bar and the bottom UI on the Shorts, TikTok and Reels players. Titles and badges are kept between y = 200 and 600.
