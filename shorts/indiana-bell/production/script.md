# Next video: "The building that turned while people worked inside" (Indiana Bell, 1930)

A follow-up to the Chicago Short: same V3 style, and the same kind of "they moved a building with people in it" hook. Target length is about 55 s (roughly 150 words).

## Voiceover script (for the owner to record)

> Imagine walking into work… and finding your entire office building has turned to face a different street.
>
> In 1930, Indianapolis had a problem.
>
> Indiana Bell wanted a bigger headquarters, right where its old eight-story telephone building stood.
>
> But that building ran the city's phone lines. Shut it down, and the calls go silent.
>
> So the architect, Kurt Vonnegut Senior, had a crazy idea. Don't tear it down. Move it.
>
> Workers slid rollers under all eleven thousand tons. Eighteen men worked the jacks, inching it along about fifteen inches an hour.
>
> First, fifty-two feet south. Then a full ninety-degree turn. Then a hundred feet west.
>
> And the wildest part? Six hundred employees kept working inside the whole time. Gas, water, power and phone lines stayed connected through flexible pipes and cables, and people walked in over a moving bridge.
>
> The phone service never stopped.
>
> And the architect's son? He grew up to be the novelist… Kurt Vonnegut.

### Recording notes
- Record in one take if you can, at a relaxed pace. Leave a beat at each "…" and at the paragraph breaks; the animation will be cut to your pauses.
- Read the numbers as words, exactly as written: "eleven thousand tons", "fifteen inches an hour".
- Hit "Move it." and "a full ninety-degree turn". Those are the visual reveals.
- If it runs long (over about 62 s), cut the "Gas, water, power…" sentence first.

## Planned visuals (V3 style, outline)
1. **Hook:** a worker at her desk looks out of the window; the street outside is slowly swinging past.
2. **Indianapolis, 1930:** a period street, an eight-story brick telephone exchange, switchboard operators.
3. **Shut it down, the calls go silent:** a switchboard with plugs and lights going dark (imagined).
4. **The architect:** Vonnegut Sr. at a drafting table, with a "MOVE IT" plan.
5. **Rollers and jacks:** men at jacks, rollers under the building, a speed card reading 15 IN / HOUR.
6. **The path:** an overhead map that animates the moves: 52 ft south, a 90° turn, 100 ft west.
7. **Still working:** operators at the switchboard, a flexible pipe flexing, people crossing a moving walkway bridge.
8. **The phone service never stopped:** a phone rings and is answered.
9. **Button:** a young boy in the architect's office looks up from a book; card: KURT VONNEGUT (novelist).
