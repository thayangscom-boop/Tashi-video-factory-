## Description
Nvidia added close to a trillion dollars in market value in a single session after the board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as President and CEO, effective immediately.

Timeline: Huang resigned on Nov 12 after reports linked him to a woman U.S. intelligence reportedly believes was a Chinese spy. On Nov 17 the board said it did not intend to reinstate him. A "Stand with Jensen" petition passed 190,000 signatures. On Nov 23 the board reversed course, saying it took input from senior levels of the U.S. government and that his return poses no national security risk. The board's investigation continues.

The twist: even after opening up about 30% (at one point 35%), NVDA is still below where it traded before the scandal, after falling more than 40% in a month. Next week Huang answers questions from Congress behind closed doors.
